ENA NS 19, f. 6 ed. Gil, Palestine, Pt. 2, pp.370-371 (Doc. #204), C.B. 01-28-88 (p) Fragment of a letter from Solomon b. Semah of Ramle to Ephraim b. Shemarya written in his own hand. (Gil) Ca. 1030
